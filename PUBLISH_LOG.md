## Known limits / not verified
- Model-year ranges on both fitment pages come from one dealer table (Leisure Time Inc.). Cross-references and sizes are from Unicel 2026. Other dealers differ at the edges (examples noted on each page).
- Hot Spring Limelight 78161 sq ft is not resolved: Unicel offers C-6350 (50) and C-6355 (75), a dealer lists 50, Pleatco PWK35B lists 35.
- No Unicel/Pleatco/Filbur match was found for Hot Spring 79435 (Tri-X), 78286, 77004; their sizes are not given in our sources, except dealer-listed Tri-X size (6" x 10-1/2", 65 sq ft).
- The 10 `needs_verification` rows were not touched (2026-10-25).
- Source data/parser: `src/data/lt-fitment.json` is parsed from the saved LT guide page.
- Jacuzzi (2026-10-26): model-years come from two non-US dealer tables (Spa World AU and the Jacuzzi Direct France table). Conflicts are listed on the page: J-235 before 2012 (6000-136 is France-only), J-270/J-280 start year (2005 vs 2007), J-LX end year (2021 vs 2022), J-465 2007 cut-over wording, J-400 end year. No public fitment table from Jacuzzi itself was found. Jacuzzi parts catalogs (2016, 2025) found online are marked CONFIDENTIAL, so they were not cited or linked. They agree with the tables used.
- Jacuzzi: no catalog aftermarket is listed for 6000-383A, 2000-498, 2540-384 or 6541-853. The 6000-383A replacements (PJW60TL-F2S/6CH-960/FC-2800) are a retailer's cross, and the catalogs list them against 6540-476 instead; the page says so.
- Sundance (2026-10-26): the main model table is Sundance Norway's (European market). LT and Snow Valley differ on 880 2005–2008 (6540-507 MicroClean Plus vs Ultra 6473-165/164) and on the 980 dates; both are shown on the page. SC817 (Berkely/Brook/Paisley 2020+) is unidentified, and 2026 780 Ultra II Lite (6473-608S) is LT-only. No aftermarket was found for Ultra II, Mini or the 6541-9xx/468 sets. Unicel's Sundance table gives C-4401 as 45 sq ft vs 35 sq ft elsewhere in Unicel.
- Flow-error guide: covers the controls whose documents we have (Gecko Y/in.xe, Balboa TP600/TP400, Sundance 780 2024, Hot Spring Highlife 2023). Other panels and older Hot Spring/Caldera displays may word their codes differently. The Balboa "5 times in 24 h" and "3 times" escalation rules come from a dealer-hosted Balboa sheet, not balboawater.com.
